Privacy Policy for Dragster Pro

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Dragster Pro ("the App," "we," "our," or "us") is a mobile racing game developed and published by OrbitForge Studios. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you download or use our App, including information processed by third-party services integrated into the App.

1. Information We Collect

OrbitForge Studios does not directly collect or maintain personal information such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or precise location information through the core gameplay of Dragster Pro. However, certain third-party services integrated into the App may automatically collect and process information as described below.

Gameplay Information

  • Gameplay progress: Game progress, scores, settings, achievements, and other gameplay information may be stored locally on your device.
  • Google Play Games Services: If enabled and used, Google Play Games Services may process player information, achievements, leaderboard scores, and cloud-save information according to Google's privacy practices.

Advertising Information

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner advertisements and rewarded video advertisements. AdMob is a third-party advertising service operated by Google.

Even when personalized advertising is disabled, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and transmit certain information, including:

  • IP address: May be used to estimate the general location of the device.
  • App interactions: Information about app launches, advertisement views, taps, and video interactions.
  • Diagnostics: Technical information about app and advertising SDK performance, including loading times, crashes, and other diagnostic information.
  • Device and account identifiers: Advertising ID, App Set ID, and other applicable device or account identifiers.

This information may be processed by Google for advertising delivery, advertising measurement, analytics, security, and fraud prevention. OrbitForge Studios does not independently collect or maintain this advertising information on its own servers.

Data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS), as described in Google's advertising data disclosure documentation.

In-App Purchase Information

If the App offers in-app purchases, transactions are processed through Google Play Billing. Google processes the relevant payment and transaction information. OrbitForge Studios does not receive or store your complete payment card details.

2. How Information Is Used

Information processed through the App and its integrated third-party services may be used for the following purposes:

  • Providing and maintaining the game's functionality.
  • Saving gameplay progress and enabling supported game services.
  • Displaying advertisements, including rewarded video advertisements.
  • Measuring advertisement performance and delivery.
  • Diagnosing technical issues and improving app stability.
  • Preventing advertising fraud and maintaining service security.
  • Processing in-app purchases and purchased content.

OrbitForge Studios does not use children's personal information for behavioral advertising, profiling, or interest-based advertising.

3. Advertising and Non-Personalized Ads

Dragster Pro uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. The App is configured to use non-personalized advertising and child-directed advertising treatment where applicable.

Non-personalized advertisements are not selected based on a user's previous browsing behavior or personal interests. However, certain technical information may still be processed by Google to deliver, measure, and protect advertising services.

Where applicable, we configure AdMob in accordance with Google's requirements for child-directed treatment and applicable advertising policies.

For additional information about Google's advertising and privacy practices, please visit:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob Data Disclosure

5. Children's Privacy

Dragster Pro may be used by children under the age of 13. We take children's privacy seriously and aim to comply with applicable children's privacy laws, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and Google Play Families Policy requirements.

  • We do not knowingly request personal information such as a child's name, email address, phone number, or precise location for core gameplay.
  • We do not use children's information for behavioral or interest-based advertising.
  • We configure advertising treatment for child users as applicable under Google's child-directed advertising requirements.
  • We do not knowingly sell children's personal information.

Parents or legal guardians who have questions or concerns about information processed through the App may contact us at support@orbitforgestudios.com.

6. Parental Rights

Parents and legal guardians may contact us to:

  • Request information about data practices associated with the App.
  • Request deletion of gameplay information maintained by us, where applicable.
  • Raise concerns about the processing of their child's information.
  • Manage purchases and parental restrictions through Google Play parental controls and Family Link.

Uninstalling the App stops its normal use on that device but does not necessarily delete information already processed by third-party services. Requests concerning information held by Google may also need to be directed to Google.

7. Data Retention and Security

Gameplay information stored locally remains on the device until it is deleted by the user or the App is uninstalled, subject to the device's storage behavior.

OrbitForge Studios does not maintain its own advertising-data database. Advertising information collected through AdMob is handled according to Google's applicable retention and privacy practices.

Third-party services may retain information for the period necessary to provide their services, comply with legal requirements, prevent fraud, and maintain security.

8. Data Sharing

OrbitForge Studios does not sell users' personal information. However, information may be transmitted to and processed by integrated third-party providers, including Google AdMob, Google Play Games Services, and Google Play Billing, where those services are enabled.

Such processing is necessary to provide advertising, game services, and purchase functionality.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in the App, third-party services, or applicable legal requirements. The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated revision date.

We encourage users and parents to review this page periodically.
