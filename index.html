Privacy Policy for Dragster Pro

Last updated: July 23, 2026

Dragster Pro ("the App," "we," "our," or "us"), developed by OrbitForge Studios, is a mobile racing game designed for players of all ages, including children. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our App, in compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy.

Information We Collect

  • Non-personal gameplay data: in-game progress, scores, settings, and achievements, stored locally or via Google Play Games Services.
  • Google Play Games Services data: if you sign in, Google may collect your Google Account ID to sync achievements, leaderboards, and cloud saves, governed by Google's own Privacy Policy.
  • Purchase data: if you make in-app purchases, transaction details are processed by Google Play Billing; we do not store your payment card information.
  • Advertising data: our App displays ads, including rewarded video ads, through third-party ad networks. For users identified as children, ads are served in a non-personalized, child-directed manner without behavioral targeting or interest-based profiling.

We do not knowingly collect precise location data, contact lists, or persistent device identifiers from children using this App.

How We Use Information

  • To operate core gameplay features, save progress, and enable cloud sync through Google Play Games Services.
  • To process in-app purchases and unlock purchased content.
  • To deliver ads, including rewarded ads that grant in-game rewards in exchange for voluntarily watching a video.

We do not use children's data for behavioral advertising, profiling, or any purpose unrelated to app functionality.

Third-Party Services

Service Purpose Data Involved
Google Play Games Services Leaderboards, achievements, cloud save Google Account ID
Google Play Billing In-app purchases Purchase/transaction info
Ad Network (e.g., AdMob) Banner/rewarded ads Non-personalized ad data for children

These third parties operate under their own privacy policies, and we require child-directed treatment settings where applicable.

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

This App may be used by children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is strictly necessary for core app functionality, and we do not permit third-party ad networks to serve personalized ads to children. Parents may review, request deletion of, or restrict further collection of their child's information by contacting us at support@orbitforgestudios.com.

Parental Rights

Parents/guardians can:

  • Request access to any data collected about their child through this App.
  • Request deletion of their child's data.
  • Withdraw consent and stop further data collection by uninstalling the App or contacting us.
  • Manage in-app purchases through Google Play's parental controls and Family Link settings.

Data Retention and Security

We retain gameplay and purchase data only as long as necessary to provide app functionality and comply with legal obligations, after which it is deleted using reasonable security measures.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy. We recommend checking this page periodically.
